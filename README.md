# Network Engineering Portfolio

**13+ years of network engineering (2013 to the present)** across an International Internet Gateway (IIG) carrier core, defense and government WANs, telecom operators and enterprise campus networks in Bangladesh. The work covers the full lifecycle: planning, design, implementation, operations, troubleshooting, upgrades and capacity planning.

> 🔒 **Confidentiality:** these write-ups describe scope, design approach and technologies only. No IP addresses, configurations, topology details or credentials from any customer network are published. Defense and government customers are described by sector, not by name. Diagrams are simplified illustrations, not the real topology.
